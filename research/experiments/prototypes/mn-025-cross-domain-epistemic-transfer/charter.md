# Gate A Charter: Milestone MN-025 — Cross-Domain Epistemic Knowledge Transfer, Cognitive Belief Conflict Reconciliation, and Stochastic Open Microworlds

## 1. Strategic Intent & Operational Scope

Milestone MN-025 evaluates the generalization frontier of small language models ($\le 4\text{B}$) in cyber-physical operational environments. Mộng Nhiễm is an operational capability platform, not an interactive game engine. Its purpose is establishing mathematically rigorous, host-authoritative cognitive architectures that allow SLMs to operate complex systems with bounded memory, zero hallucinations, and provable safety invariants.

MN-025 evaluates three sequential research stages executed on a single local open-weights model (`Qwen3.5-2B-Q4_K_M.gguf`):
1. **Stage 1 (Cross-Domain Epistemic Knowledge Transfer):** Structural causal isomorphism ($f: \mathcal{G}_{\text{src}} \to \mathcal{G}_{\text{tgt}}$) across disjoint physical vocabularies, featuring negative transfer and latent cavitation/resonance degradation traps.
2. **Stage 2 (Cognitive Belief Conflict Reconciliation):** Dialectic belief convergence between asymmetric agent perspectives, tracking graph divergence ($d(\mathcal{G}_A, \mathcal{G}_B) \to 0$) and tie-breaking stubborn deadlock and unexpressed assumption traps.
3. **Stage 3 (Stochastic Shocks & Dynamic Topology):** Resilience to non-linear Poisson shocks and zero-shot utilization of dynamic entity affordances, subjected to phantom entity decoys, latent thermal runaway sleepers, and non-monotonic anchor demotion.

## 2. Formal Research Questions

- **$Q_1$ (Isomorphism Transfer):** Can an SLM transfer an abstract 4-hop causal mitigation template ($\text{Detect} \to \text{Isolate} \to \text{Reroute} \to \text{Stabilize}$) from a familiar Source Domain to unfamiliar Target Domains, achieving $\ge 40\%$ faster mitigation than cold-start exploration?
- **$Q_2$ (Negative Transfer Interception):** Can the Host Authoritative Guard intercept $100\%$ of erroneous domain analogies before physical conservation invariants are violated?
- **$Q_3$ (Dialectic Reconciliation):** Can two asymmetric agent viewpoints converge to an objective causal consensus through structured dialectic exchange without encountering infinite deadlock cycles?
- **$Q_4$ (Latent Trap Detection):** Can the cognitive architecture identify latent degradation (cavitation micro-erosion, phase drift, unexpressed assumptions, thermal runaway sleepers) before catastrophic time-bomb triggers explode?
- **$Q_5$ (Dynamic Affordance Utilization):** Can an SLM utilize newly registered dynamic entities zero-shot based purely on Typed Affordance Signatures?
- **$Q_6$ (Non-Monotonic Epistemic Revision):** Can the Host purge and tombstone falsified Tier 1 anchors when unexpected regime shifts occur, eliminating cognitive inertia?

## 3. Formal Research Hypotheses & Falsification Criteria

### Hypothesis 1: Cross-Domain Transfer Speedup ($H_1$)
$$\Delta T = \frac{T_{\text{cold}} - T_{\text{transferred}}}{T_{\text{cold}}} \ge 40.0\%$$
- **Falsification Criterion:** $\Delta T < 40.0\%$ over $N=20$ Stage 1 evaluation cases.

### Hypothesis 2: Zero Negative Transfer Invariant Breaches ($H_2$)
$$B_{\text{negative}}(\text{Arm 3}) \equiv 0 \quad \text{and} \quad B_{\text{latent}}(\text{Arm 3}) \equiv 0$$
- **Falsification Criterion:** Any committed physical breach resulting from negative transfer or latent degradation traps ($B > 0$).

### Hypothesis 3: Dialectic Conflict Convergence ($H_3$)
$$C_{\text{dialectic}} = \frac{N_{\text{converged}}}{N_{\text{conflict}}} \ge 90.0\% \quad \text{with} \quad d(\mathcal{G}_A, \mathcal{G}_B) \to 0$$
- **Falsification Criterion:** Dialectic convergence falls below $90.0\%$ or produces persistent deadlock cycles.

### Hypothesis 4: Bounded Prompt Memory Ceiling ($H_4$)
$$\max_{t \in [1, T]} \text{Tokens}_t(\text{Arm 3}) \le 512\text{ tokens}$$
- **Falsification Criterion:** Peak working prompt size exceeds $512$ tokens at any turn.

### Hypothesis 5: Stochastic Shock Recovery ($H_5$)
$$T_{\text{recovery}} \le 6\text{ ticks} \quad \text{and} \quad S_{\text{affordance}} \ge 85.0\%$$
- **Falsification Criterion:** Mean recovery from Poisson step shocks exceeds $6$ ticks, or zero-shot dynamic affordance utilization falls below $85.0\%$.

### Hypothesis 6: Non-Monotonic Anchor Demotion Fidelity ($H_6$)
$$D_{\text{demotion}} \equiv 100.0\%$$
- **Falsification Criterion:** Any falsified Tier 1 rule is retained or re-executed following an environmental regime shift.

## 5. Experimental Arms & Controlled Variables

- **Arm 1 (Monolithic Cold-Start Baseline):** Solitary unspecialized agent without causal schemas or phased roles.
- **Arm 2 (Phased Control Baseline):** Phased Scout-Actuator execution without cross-domain schema projection or dynamic schema registries.
- **Arm 3 (Host Topological Epistemic Transfer Engine):** Phased Scout-Actuator execution armed with Topological Schema Cards, Epistemic Reconciler, Dynamic Schema Registry, and Invariant Guards.

All arms are evaluated across the identical $N=60$ test scenario corpus under deterministic temperature $T=0.0$ on local `Qwen3.5-2B-Q4_K_M.gguf`.
